WELCOME TO BLUEHAVEN

You’re the steward of this coast.

For eight years, choose one policy each year. Watch fish stocks, water quality, local livelihoods, and community wellbeing respond.

  • Limit fishing: give fish a chance to recover, with a short-term cost to livelihoods.
  • Reduce pollution: invest in cleaner water and healthier communities.
  • Protect marine habitats: establish reserves with lasting benefits.
  • Take no action: keep earnings today, while pressure on the ocean builds.

Compare your final coast with a “no action” future. Can you balance immediate needs with long-term recovery?

This deterministic teaching model uses illustrative scores, not scientific predictions. Policy effects apply for one year; reserves persist (up to three). No account or payment required.
